# Price War Game · Privacy policy

Price War Game is a student project for the course Introduction to Programming at Nova SBE.

## What we store
- Your Google account email and name, to know which game is yours.
- Your game data: cash, stock, prices, loans, days played and results, in practice runs and tournaments.

We never see or store your Google password: sign-in is handled by Google.

## Who sees your data
- Your practice game is private to you.
- In a tournament, the other players of that tournament see your name, your prices and your units sold. They never see your cash or your stock.

## Services we use
- Turso stores the game data.
- When a practice run ends, the game emails you your result from the team's Gmail account. The email goes only to you.
- The weather comes from Open-Meteo. No data about you is sent.

Your data is not sold or used for advertising.

## How long
The data is kept until the end of the course project, and then deleted.
